How decisions are made

The AI never decides. It proposes; plain code checks the proposal against four rules, and each rule is tied to UAE law. AI advisers give opinions, and a person approves anything unclear.

What we propose to ITU

ITU-T SG13 · Y.3172

Add a trust and provenance label for data where it is collected, a quarantine step before the AI model, and a policy step with cited rules for any action that has an outside effect.

ITU-T SG17 · security

A standard test for planted instructions against network AI agents. We offer our 24 attacks, adaptive attacks and 36 blind cases as a starting benchmark, plus an identity and mandate scheme for official AI agents.

AI Readiness · D10

A new measure: the share of AI-proposed actions that pass a policy check with a cited rule, and the rate at which planted text fools the AI in a sandbox test.

Proof

Every number comes from recorded runs, and every step is sealed in an audit log you can check yourself.

Experiment 1: did a planted line in the data fool a real AI agent (HolmesGPT) about the cause of a fault? 72 attacked runs per set-up. Experiment 2: did our rules stop harmful actions without blocking normal work?

Replay a recorded run: same attack, without and with the trust layer

Experiment 2: policy enforcement

Every step is sealed into a chain: each entry holds the fingerprint (SHA-256) of the one before, so changing any entry breaks it. Verify checks the chain with an independent program; Tamper test changes a copy to prove the check works. The attacker's original text is stored encrypted and referenced by its fingerprint.

A hash chain proves the record was not changed after it was written. It does not prove the record is true.

Limits, stated plainly

  • Two AI models from one vendor; a synthetic network.
  • We fixed two holes our own attacks found, then ran a blind test: 20/20 attacks stopped, 2 of 16 normal requests wrongly held.
  • UAE legal citations are a first pass; the team's legal check is in progress.
  • Messages between agents are not yet signed or encrypted in transit (all agents run in one process).
