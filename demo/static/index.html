Audit & forensics

Every step is written to an append-only, hash-chained log. Source text is encrypted and referenced by its fingerprint. The check below runs tools/verify_audit.py, which imports nothing from our code.

A hash chain proves the record was not changed after it was written. It does not prove the record is true.
